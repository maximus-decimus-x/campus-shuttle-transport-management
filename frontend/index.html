ABOUT THE SYSTEM

One platform for campus transportation.

Campus Shuttle is a university transport management system designed to organize transportation services through a centralized platform.

Students and faculty can access shuttle information, reserve seats and submit complaints, while administrators manage vehicles, drivers, routes and schedules.
